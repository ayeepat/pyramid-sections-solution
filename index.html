Sections of a quadrilateral pyramid

Answers:
(a) section K–E–F–N–M
(b) section P–R–U–Q–T

The pyramid is SABCD. The worksheet uses the letter S twice: for the apex and for the red point on edge CD. On this page, the red point is called Q. Therefore the worksheet’s plane (PRS) is written here as (PRQ).

Main rule: a cutting plane intersects each face in a straight line. Two known points in the same face determine that line. Lines may be extended outside the pyramid to find auxiliary intersections.

(a) Plane (KMN)

Answer: pentagon KEFNM.

Construction

  1. Draw KM and MN. Points K and M lie on face SAD. Points M and N lie on face SCD. Therefore these segments are sides of the section.
  2. Extend MK beyond K and DA beyond A. Mark their intersection: X = KM ∩ AD.
  3. Extend MN beyond N and DC beyond C. Mark Y = MN ∩ CD.
  4. Draw XY. Points X and Y lie in both the cutting plane and the base plane, so XY is the line where those planes intersect.
  5. Mark E = XY ∩ AB and F = XY ∩ BC.
  6. Join the section in order: K → E → F → N → M → K.

The section sides lie on these faces: KE on SAB, EF on the base ABCD, FN on SBC, NM on SCD, and MK on SAD.

Common mistake: do not add a vertex on SB. In this drawing, the cutting plane meets the extension of SB below B, outside the edge.

(b) Plane (PRQ)

Answer: pentagon PRUQT.

Construction

  1. Draw PR. Both points lie on face SAB, so PR is one side of the section.
  2. Extend PR beyond R and AB beyond B. Mark X = PR ∩ AB.
  3. Draw XQ. Points X and Q lie in both the cutting plane and the base plane, so XQ is the line where those planes intersect.
  4. Mark U = XQ ∩ BC. Join R to U.
  5. Extend XQ beyond Q and AD beyond D. Mark W = XQ ∩ AD.
  6. Draw PW and mark T = PW ∩ SD.
  7. Join T to Q. The completed section is P → R → U → Q → T → P.

The section sides lie on these faces: PR on SAB, RU on SBC, UQ on the base ABCD, QT on SCD, and TP on SAD.

How to draw the final figures

Use a separate copy of the pyramid for each part. Draw auxiliary lines thinly. Draw the section boundary with a thicker line and lightly shade its interior. Following the worksheet’s visibility convention, draw KM in part (a) and PT in part (b) with dashed lines because they lie on the hidden face SAD. The points outside the pyramid are construction aids, not vertices of the section.
